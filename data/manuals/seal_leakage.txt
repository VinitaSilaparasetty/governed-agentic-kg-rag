SECTION 1: MECHANICAL SEAL BASICS
A mechanical seal prevents fluid escaping along the pump shaft. It consists of a rotating
face (attached to shaft) and a stationary face (in housing), held in contact by a spring.
The extremely thin film of fluid between faces provides lubrication and creates the seal.
If the faces separate, run dry, or become contaminated, leakage occurs.

SECTION 2: CAUSES OF SEAL FAILURE
- Dry running: seal faces overheat within seconds without lubricating fluid — highest
  single cause of premature failure. Ensure pump is primed before start.
- Vibration: excessive vibration (>4.5 mm/s at seal housing) causes dynamic face
  separation. Resolve vibration root cause before fitting new seal.
- Misalignment: shaft runout >0.05 mm TIR causes cyclic face separation.
- Contamination: abrasive particles in process fluid erode seal faces; use appropriate
  seal material (SiC vs SiC for abrasive service; Carbon vs SiC for clean service).
- Thermal shock: starting pump with cold seal into hot process causes face cracking.

SECTION 3: DIAGNOSIS
Seal leakage rate > 10 drops/minute indicates failure. Inspect seal gland area for:
- Crystallised deposits (slow weep with evaporation)
- Active drips or spray (face separation or spring failure)
- Discolouration/burns on shaft sleeve (dry-run indicator)

SECTION 4: CARTRIDGE SEAL REPLACEMENT
1. Depressurise and drain pump casing; verify 0 bar on gauge.
2. Issue LOTO permit.
3. Disconnect flush and quench piping from seal gland.
4. Remove impeller (anti-clockwise thread on most centrifugal pumps — check manual).
5. Slide old seal cartridge off shaft; inspect shaft sleeve for scoring.
6. Measure shaft sleeve OD — replace sleeve if OD is >0.1 mm undersize.
7. Wipe shaft and housing bore clean; apply a film of clean process fluid or silicone
   grease to shaft sleeve (never petroleum grease on elastomers).
8. Slide new cartridge seal into position; remove anti-rotation pins after fitting.
9. Reinstall impeller; set correct impeller clearance (0.3–0.5 mm for open impellers).
10. Reconnect flush lines; open flush valve before starting pump.
11. Perform pressure test at 1.5× operating pressure for 15 minutes; check for weeps.

SECTION 5: ENHANCED MONITORING
After seal replacement, monitor shaft vibration daily for 7 days. Leakage above 5
drops/minute within the first 48 hours indicates installation defect — do not run to
failure; shut down and re-inspect.
