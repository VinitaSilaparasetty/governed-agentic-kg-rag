SECTION 1: TEST RIG DESCRIPTION
The dataset captures multi-sensor readings from a hydraulic test rig with a primary and
secondary working circuit. The rig operates repeated 60-second load cycles. Four hydraulic
components are monitored simultaneously, each with independently varied condition grades
spanning the full range from nominal to severely degraded. The test rig simulates the
operating conditions of industrial hydraulic systems used in manufacturing, press systems,
and mobile machinery.

SECTION 2: SENSOR CHANNELS (17 SENSORS)
Pressure sensors (6):
  PS1: 100 Hz, 6,000 samples/cycle — system pressure at primary circuit inlet
  PS2: 100 Hz — primary working pressure
  PS3: 100 Hz — secondary circuit pressure
  PS4: 100 Hz — return line pressure
  PS5: 100 Hz — pump supply pressure
  PS6: 100 Hz — pump return pressure

Flow sensors (2):
  FS1: 10 Hz, 600 samples/cycle — volumetric flow, primary circuit
  FS2: 10 Hz — volumetric flow, secondary circuit

Temperature sensors (4):
  TS1: 1 Hz, 60 samples/cycle — hydraulic fluid temperature at reservoir
  TS2: 1 Hz — temperature at primary circuit
  TS3: 1 Hz — temperature at secondary circuit
  TS4: 1 Hz — temperature at cooler outlet

Vibration sensor (1):
  VS1: 1 Hz — vibration of the pump body (overall RMS, broadband)

Motor power sensor (1):
  EPS1: 100 Hz — electrical power consumption of the drive motor

SECTION 3: FAULT TYPES AND GRADED SEVERITY LABELS
The dataset provides class labels for four hydraulic component conditions,
each with multiple discrete severity grades:

3.1 PUMP INTERNAL LEAKAGE
  Condition grades: no leakage (nominal) / weak leakage / severe leakage
  Observable signatures:
    - PS1 inlet pressure: normal
    - FS1 volumetric flow: deficit relative to expected (>5% deficit = leakage path)
    - TS1 fluid temperature: elevated due to recirculating fluid energy dissipation
    - EPS1 motor power: slightly elevated (motor compensates for volumetric loss)
  KG mapping: Seal Leakage (F-SL-03) — internal seal bypass causes flow discrepancy

3.2 HYDRAULIC VALVE CONDITION
  Condition grades: optimal (100%) / slight degradation (90%) / moderate (80%) / severe (73%)
  Observable signatures:
    - Time lag between control signal change and FS2 response > 0.3 s (normal: < 0.1 s)
    - Irregular pressure spikes at PS4 during switching cycles
    - PS2-PS3 pressure differential anomaly during valve transition
  KG mapping: Valve Seat Erosion (F-VS-10) — erosion reduces seating force and causes
    internal bypass; Actuator Sticking (F-AS-09) — degraded switching reflects
    diaphragm or spring resistance change

3.3 COOLER EFFICIENCY
  Condition grades: full efficiency (100%) / reduced (20%) / close to failure (3%)
  Observable signatures:
    - TS4 outlet temperature: elevated above nominal cooling differential
    - Temperature rise proportional to blockage severity
  KG mapping: Filter Fouling (F-FF-08) — fouled heat exchanger or filter causes
    temperature rise and reduced system efficiency (conceptually analogous)

3.4 ACCUMULATOR PRESSURE
  Condition grades: optimal (130 bar) / slightly reduced (115 bar) / degraded (100 bar) / close to failure (90 bar)
  Observable signatures:
    - PS5 pump supply pressure: reduced during high-demand transients
    - Increased pressure variance during load switching cycles

SECTION 4: DATASET STATISTICS
  Total load cycles (instances): 2,205
  Total features: 43,680 data points across all sensor channels per cycle
  Multi-label: all four component conditions are labelled independently per cycle
  No missing values; balanced across condition grades

SECTION 5: DIAGNOSTIC PATTERNS RELEVANT TO THE KG-RAG SYSTEM
The UCI dataset demonstrates two patterns directly relevant to the system:

A) SENSOR CORRELATION FOR PUMP LEAKAGE DETECTION:
   FS1 (flow) vs FS2 discrepancy combined with TS1 temperature rise
   → confirms internal bypass before visible external leakage occurs
   This supports the KG→RAG fusion pattern: KG identifies 'Seal Leakage' from symptom
   keywords; RAG retrieves sensor thresholds (FS1-FS2 > 5%, TS1 rise > 3°C) for
   procedure-level diagnostic guidance.

B) TIME-SERIES FEATURE FOR VALVE FAULT:
   Valve switching lag (time domain feature from FS2 trace)
   → directly diagnostic of valve condition grade without frequency-domain transform
   This supports RQ4 (multi-modal neuro-symbolic fusion): valve condition could be
   inferred from a transformer-based time-series model outputting to the KG agent.
