SECTION 1: TEST RIG DESCRIPTION
The CWRU bearing fault test rig comprises a 2 HP Reliance Electric motor driving a
shaft supported by two test bearings. A dynamometer/torque transducer applies mechanical
load at the driven end. The rig operates at four load levels (0–3 HP) corresponding to
shaft speeds of 1797, 1772, 1750, and 1730 RPM. Vibration data are collected by
accelerometers mounted at the 12 o'clock position on the motor housing, one at the
drive end and one at the fan end. Sampling rate: 12,000 or 48,000 samples per second
depending on the sub-dataset.

SECTION 3: CHARACTERISTIC FAULT FREQUENCY FORMULAS
Fault frequencies are computed from the bearing geometry and expressed as multiples
of shaft rotation frequency (1x). Standard formulas:

  BPFO (Ball Pass Frequency, Outer Race) = (Nb/2) * (1 - Bd/Pd * cos(alpha))
  BPFI (Ball Pass Frequency, Inner Race) = (Nb/2) * (1 + Bd/Pd * cos(alpha))
  BSF  (Ball Spin Frequency)             = (Pd/2*Bd) * (1 - (Bd/Pd * cos(alpha))^2)
  FTF  (Fundamental Train Frequency)     = (1/2)   * (1 - Bd/Pd * cos(alpha))

SECTION 4: COMPUTED FAULT FREQUENCY MULTIPLES
Drive-End Bearing (SKF 6205-2RS) at contact angle = 0 deg:
  BPFO:  3.585 x shaft frequency   (outer race fault signature)
  BPFI:  5.415 x shaft frequency   (inner race fault signature)
  BSF:   2.357 x shaft frequency   (rolling element fault signature)
  FTF:   0.398 x shaft frequency   (cage fault signature)

Fan-End Bearing (SKF 6203-2RS) at contact angle = 0 deg:
  BPFO:  3.053 x shaft frequency
  BPFI:  4.947 x shaft frequency
  BSF:   1.994 x shaft frequency
  FTF:   0.382 x shaft frequency

Example at 1750 RPM (shaft frequency = 29.17 Hz), Drive-End:
  BPFO = 3.585 x 29.17 Hz = 104.6 Hz — dominant spectral peak for outer race fault
  BPFI = 5.415 x 29.17 Hz = 158.0 Hz — dominant spectral peak for inner race fault
  BSF  = 2.357 x 29.17 Hz =  68.7 Hz — dominant spectral peak for rolling element fault

SECTION 5: FAULT CONDITIONS AND SEVERITY LEVELS
Seeded faults were introduced at three locations using electro-discharge machining (EDM):
  Locations: inner raceway, outer raceway, rolling element (ball)
  Fault diameters (EDM pit): 0.007", 0.014", 0.021", 0.028" (and 0.040" for some sets)

Typical vibration amplitudes by fault size (drive-end, 1750 RPM, 1 HP load):
  Normal (no fault):       RMS velocity < 1.0 mm/s   — ISO 10816-3 Zone A
  0.007" fault (early):    RMS velocity approx 2-4 mm/s — Zone B/C onset
  0.014" fault (moderate): RMS velocity approx 4-7 mm/s — Zone C
  0.028" fault (severe):   RMS velocity > 7 mm/s       — Zone D (shutdown)
  (Values from published spectral analyses of CWRU data; see Smith & Randall 2015.)

SECTION 6: MAPPING TO KG FAULT TAXONOMY
CWRU fault condition            → KG FaultType          → fault_code
Inner race fault (BPFI peaks)   → Bearing Wear           → F-BW-01
Outer race fault (BPFO peaks)   → Bearing Wear           → F-BW-01
Rolling element fault (BSF)     → Bearing Wear           → F-BW-01
Fault severity 0.007"-0.014"    → KG severity: MEDIUM
Fault severity 0.021"-0.040"    → KG severity: HIGH

The symptom attributes in the KG FaultType node — 'excessive vibration', 'high temperature',
'noise' — directly correspond to the observable correlates of the BPFO/BPFI/BSF spectral
patterns documented in the CWRU dataset. As fault diameter increases, broadband noise rises
and bearing temperature increases due to metal-to-metal contact in the spalled zone, confirming
the symptom-to-fault mapping in the KG schema.
